# Privacy notice

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Envato XPerience is an independent Chrome extension maintained by [Mauricio Vuljevas](https://github.com/mvuljevas). This notice describes the extension's current source code and browsing features.

## Information used

The extension reads the current page address and relevant Envato product information, such as the title, product links, image, price and rating, to show its panel and preserve links when opening live demos. On downloads pages, the optional removed-items feature reads visible item details and existing license links. It does not request or store your Envato password, purchase products, or grant access to unavailable files.

The extension has no developer-operated backend, analytics, advertising identifiers or telemetry. It does not sell browsing information or transmit product context to the maintainer. Page information is used only for the browsing features described in the user guide, consistent with the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements.

## Storage

Feature preferences are stored through Chrome's synchronized settings storage and may sync through your browser account, according to your browser settings. Product context is saved locally to reconnect a live demo with its Envato links; the widget uses context from the previous two hours. Context is overwritten by later previews rather than automatically erased at that cutoff.

Product images may be cached in the browser. Cache entries expire after seven days and are pruned when the cache is updated, with limits on entry count and total size. Some cached data and setting mirrors are stored in the marketplace site's browser storage; other data belongs to the extension. The extension does not read or upload your complete browsing history.

## Website access and external services

Broad website access supports demos hosted on different authors' domains, the floating widget and the in-page panel. Envato pages, demo sites and product image hosts receive normal browser requests when you visit pages or load images. Their own privacy practices apply.

Support links open GitHub Sponsors, Ko-fi or PayPal only when selected. Those services handle their own accounts, payments and privacy practices. The extension does not process payment details. Optional contributions do not change access to its features.

## Control and removal

You can turn off optional features in Admin, restrict site access through Chrome's extension settings, or uninstall the extension. Restricting site access can prevent demos or the panel from working. Chrome manages extension-owned data when the extension is removed. Marketplace site data, including local setting mirrors and cached images written there, may remain until you clear that site's data through Chrome. Clearing site data can also sign you out of that website.

## Questions and changes

Use the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/mvuljevas/EnvatoXperience/issues) for questions, without posting credentials or private account information. Changes to this notice will be published with the source code and its update date. GitHub, Google and other services manage data you submit directly to them under their own policies.
